Aerodynamic Wind Turbines for Onshore & Offshore Clean Power

Deploying low cut-in speed wind technology, advanced composite carbon fiber blades, and low-vibration Vertical Axis Wind Turbines (VAWT) for continuous baseload power.

Turbine Architecture

Dual Rotor Configurations: HAWT & VAWT

Stackly engineers both Horizontal Axis and Vertical Axis wind systems to accommodate diverse environmental topologies.

Urban & Industrial

Vertical Axis Wind Turbines (VAWT)

Omnidirectional design captures turbulent and multi-directional winds without needing yaw control motors. Operates with near-silent acoustics, ideal for rooftop and suburban campuses.

  • Cut-in wind speed: Just 1.8 m/s (4 mph)
  • Generator positioned at base for simple ground-level maintenance
  • Acoustic noise below 38 dBA at 10 meters
  • Zero avian collision hazards due to dense visual profile
Utility Scale

Horizontal Axis Wind Turbines (HAWT)

High-capacity megawatt-scale turbines designed for coastal ridges, open plains, and offshore continental shelves, delivering highest capacity factor.

  • Individual turbine ratings: 2.5 MW to 8.0 MW
  • Pitch-regulated carbon composite blades up to 85m length
  • Direct-drive permanent magnet synchronous generators
  • Availability factor: 99.4% through predictive vibration telemetry
Offshore Engineering

Offshore Continental Shelf Deployment

Offshore winds provide sustained, high-density kinetic power. Stackly handles monopile and jacket foundation civil works, subsea array cabling, and corrosion-resistant nacelles.

  • ✓ Marine-grade C5-M anti-corrosion coating on towers and nacelles
  • ✓ Pressurized internal dehumidification systems protecting electronics
  • ✓ Subsea 33 kV and 66 kV export cable laying with fiber-optic monitoring
  • ✓ Remote SCADA helicopter landing decks and crew transfer access
Atmospheric Science

LiDAR Laser Anemometry & Predictive Yaw

Maximizing megawatt output by measuring wind velocity and wake deflection hundreds of meters ahead of blade contact.

📡

Nacelle-Mounted LiDAR

Scans wind speed and turbulence 300 meters upstream, allowing the blade pitch to adjust before wind gusts arrive.

🌪️

Wake Steering Optimization

Slightly deflects upstream turbine yaw to direct wind shadows away from downstream turbines, increasing farm output by 4.2%.

🛡️

Ultrasonic Bat & Bird Deterrence

Automated acoustic radar pulses harmless frequencies that steer migrating birds away from rotor swept areas.
