Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions Regarding Renewable Deployment

How long does a commercial solar or wind installation typically take?

C&I rooftop solar installations usually require 8 to 14 weeks from permit approvals to utility commissioning. Utility wind and solar farms range from 6 to 14 months depending on site civil works.

What warranties and performance guarantees does Stackly provide?

We provide 25-year linear power output warranties on photovoltaic panels, 15-year warranties on wind turbines, 10-year / 6,000-cycle warranties on BESS systems, and a 99.8% inverter uptime SLA.

Can Stackly's system function during an electric grid blackout?

Yes. When paired with our Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), the microgrid switches to islanding mode within 16 milliseconds, sustaining mission-critical loads without interruption.
