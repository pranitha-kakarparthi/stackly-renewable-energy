Operational Roles

Four Pillars of BESS Value Creation

Unlocking multiple revenue and savings streams through flexible battery dispatch algorithms.

⚡

Peak Shaving

Discharges stored solar/wind electricity during expensive peak utility rate windows, slashing billing demand spikes.

⏱️

Frequency Regulation

Injects or absorbs power in under 20 milliseconds to stabilize grid frequency at 50/60 Hz, earning ancillary grid service revenue.

🔄

Energy Arbitrage

Charges batteries when wholesale grid prices are low (or negative) and discharges during evening scarcity hours.

🛡️

Black-Start Resilience

Provides instant grid-forming voltage reference during major blackout events, maintaining critical plant operations.
