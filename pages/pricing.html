Financing FAQ

Frequently Asked Financing Questions

What is a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA)?

Under a PPA, Stackly finances, installs, owns, and maintains the entire solar and battery storage system on your property with $0 upfront investment. You simply buy the clean power generated at a predictable rate significantly lower than utility retail tariffs.

Can we buy out the system during the PPA term?

Yes. Standard Stackly PPA agreements include fair market buyout options starting after Year 5, allowing your company to transition to full asset ownership at any time.
